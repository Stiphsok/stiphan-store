About STIPHAN STORE

STIPHAN STORE is a small web game storefront built around STIPHAN Brick Fire and a collection of arcade, racing, puzzle, and strategy games that run in a modern browser.

What you can do here

  • Play the built-in browser games without creating an account.
  • Browse game themes, save favorites, and keep optional virtual points in this browser.
  • Download the Android packages listed in the Apps section.

Virtual STC points are for on-site features only. They are not money, a payment method, or redeemable for cash.

The owner has not supplied a public business identity or contact address. Those details and all distribution rights should be confirmed before a public commercial launch.
