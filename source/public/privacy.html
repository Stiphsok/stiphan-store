Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current STIPHAN STORE website. It should be reviewed and updated by the owner before introducing analytics, advertising, or other data services.

Information stored in your browser

The site stores optional preferences such as favorites, selected themes, and virtual STC points in local browser storage. These values stay in that browser unless you clear its site data. They are not an account balance and have no cash value.

Advertising and cookies

Advertising and third-party analytics are not enabled on the public store. The site does not currently load the AdSense script. No advertising cookies are needed for the current experience. If the project is configured for Supabase, the client may check an existing administrator session. Before enabling advertising or analytics, the owner must update this policy and provide any consent choices required for visitors' locations.

Downloads and external services

App packages are delivered as downloads from this site's host. Visiting or downloading from the site may create standard technical logs at the hosting provider. If a separate upload feature is configured to use Supabase, files submitted through that feature may be sent to that configured service.

Contact and policy updates

No public privacy contact address has been supplied. Add an owner-managed contact method and identify the responsible data controller before launch. This policy must be updated if the site's data practices change.
